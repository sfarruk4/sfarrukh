About me

Who I am, in one breath.

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at the University of Western Ontario, graduating in April 2027. Over two co-op terms at Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Regional Jets, I worked at the seam where engineering meets operations: coordinating heavy-maintenance projects across engineering, supply chain, and procurement, and building the data reporting that leadership relies on to keep a global fleet flying.

The habits that make me useful on a project come straight off the wrestling mat — showing up, doing the unglamorous reps, and staying calm when the pressure is on. Away from work I build software — from a Java educational game to a Python flight-routing tool to co-founding Nera, an on-demand marketplace for local work — and I like shipping things people can actually use.
